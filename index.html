Most dental problems are a symptom of something.

A cavity, a cracked molar, gums that bleed, a jaw that aches in the morning. Fill it, crown it, and it comes back, because nothing addressed why it happened.

First, we look properly

An examination that is actually an examination

Digital X-rays and an intraoral camera tour, so you see your own teeth on the screen rather than take our word for anything. Full periodontal charting, six points per tooth. Oral cancer screening. How your teeth meet and what that is doing to them. And a real conversation about how you sleep and breathe, because clenching, worn teeth and airway problems travel together far more often than people expect.

You leave with a written plan and the reasoning behind it. Not a quote for the most expensive option, a plan you can stage over months if that suits your life better.

  • Digital X-rays and an intraoral camera walkthrough
  • Gum, bite, airway and oral cancer screening
  • A written plan, with costs and coverage confirmed first
Then we make it bearable

Built for people who do not like the dentist

Dental anxiety is the reason most people give for a ten year gap, so we removed as many of the triggers as we could. The chairs use gentle acoustic frequency to settle the nervous system. There are noise cancelling headphones, TV goggles, foam pillows and stress balls, and nobody here will make you feel bad about how long it has been.

For anything longer or more involved, Dr. Silver is certified in IV sedation. A wisdom tooth, an implant or a full day of work can happen while you rest through it.

  • Neuro-Spa chairs, headphones, TV goggles and weighted comfort
  • IV sedation available with Dr. Max Silver
  • A stop signal agreed before we start, that we actually honour

Good to know

Questions people ask us

What makes Oceana Dental different from other dental clinics in Ottawa?
We start every new patient with a comprehensive exam that looks past the teeth. We assess your bite, your gums, your airway and how you breathe and sleep, then we look at how those connect to the rest of your health. Most dental problems have a cause somewhere in that picture. We would rather find it than keep patching the result.
What is wellness dentistry and is it evidence based?
Wellness dentistry means we treat the mouth as part of the body rather than a separate system. It is built on mainstream, published dentistry, not alternative practice. Dr. Gaudet has trained at the Kois Center in Seattle, the University of Pennsylvania, the Misch International Implant Institute and the Breathe Institute, and holds IAOMT SMART certification for safe amalgam removal. We use the same diagnostics any strong general practice uses, we simply read them in a wider context.
Do you accept the Canada Dental Care Plan?
Yes. We accept CDCP patients and we will tell you exactly what your plan covers and what, if anything, is left over before any treatment starts. If you are still working out whether you qualify, call us and we will walk you through it.
How much does a first visit cost?
It depends on what your exam needs and what coverage you have. We check your insurance or CDCP benefits before your appointment and give you the numbers in writing before we do anything. You will never be surprised by an invoice here.
I have not been to a dentist in years. Will I get a lecture?
No. A good number of our patients come to us after a long gap, often because a previous experience went badly. We start where you are. You will get a clear picture of what is going on and a plan you can take in stages if that suits you better.
Do you see children and families?
Yes, we see every age. Dr. Silver in particular is very good with nervous young patients. He is a voice actor outside the practice and is not above using a few impressions to get a child through their first visit.
Where are you and is it easy to get to?
We are at 115 Champagne Avenue South in Ottawa, just off Carling near Dow's Lake, which puts us within easy reach of the Glebe, Little Italy, Hintonburg, Nepean and the Gatineau side of the river.
What are your hours?
Monday 10:00 am to 7:00 pm, Tuesday to Thursday 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. We are closed Friday, Saturday and Sunday. The Monday evening block is there for people who cannot take time off during the day.
