Before you come in

Three things worth knowing.

Coverage sorted first

Send your insurance or CDCP details when you book and we confirm what is covered before you sit in the chair. No treatment starts without a written estimate.

Set aside ninety minutes

A first visit is a full assessment, not a quick look. If you cannot spare that, tell us and we will find another way to get you started.

Tell us if you are nervous

Genuinely. Say it in the notes box. We will book more time, go slower, and set the room up for you before you walk in.
